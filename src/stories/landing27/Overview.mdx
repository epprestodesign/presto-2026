# Landing Page Concepts '27

> **Concept only.** An EventPipe / Presto design exploration that uses the
> **IMEX America 2027** brand as a starting point. Not affiliated with or
> endorsed by IMEX. Hotel rates, room-block numbers and organizations are
> sample data.

## Why

From the **Weekly Check In (Nov 2, 2026)** with Christine:

- Build **Presto homepage variants for the corporate events vertical**:
  conferences, meeting planners, CVBs and big group travel. Corporate clients
  care more about design than sports clients do.
- Aim for **3–5 brand templates** as conversation starters for when Josh takes
  them to customers.
- **IMEX** is the reference. Its audience is meeting planners, CVBs and
  non-sports clients who need hotel blocks.
- Repurpose the booking widget: **the team selector becomes a company /
  department field**.

## The five concepts

Each concept reaches the booking widget a different way, so we can compare them side by side.

| Concept | Look | How guests reach booking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Heartbeat** | Closest to IMEX's own site; light and editorial | Widget **pinned to the hero**, plus a **sticky top bar** after scrolling |
| **After Dark** | Dark, cinematic, oversized type | **Floating "Book your hotel" badge** and hero CTA open the **booking modal** |
| **Planner's Desk** | Corporate and practical | **Corporate widget in the hero**, attendee-type tabs, rate comparison, group-block request |
| **Design Matters** | Bold geometric tiles in IMEX colors | **Schedule → your stay** planner opens booking in a **side panel** |
| **Agenda First** | Editorial conference site | **"Book for this day"** on every agenda day, a **sticky bottom bar**, and a widget in the venue section |

## Shared components

Every component sits under **Components**, uses the brand layer (`.l27`) and is aimed at getting guests to book:

- **Booking Widget (corporate).** Company / organization, Attending as
  (Exhibitor, Hosted buyer, Buyer / planner, Speaker, Media, Staff), an
  event-aware **Your stay** picker with Smart Monday and the show days
  marked, and Rooms & guests. It has a *bar* layout and a *stacked* layout.
- **Booking Modal.** Shows the form, then "Finding rooms near Mandalay
  Bay…", then a results preview. Built on the design system's DsModal.
- **Stay Planner.** Pick the days you'll attend and it works out check-in and check-out.
- **Hotels & Map.** Walk time to the venue, block rate vs. public rate, rooms
  left, and pins on a stylized map of the Strip.
- **Countdown**, **Stats Band**, **Block Meter**, **Sticky Bar**, **Floating
  Button**, **Logo Strip**, **FAQ** (including the anti-scam housing note), and
  **Add to Calendar**.

## Putting booking on outside templates (Framer, Webflow, Squarespace…)

Many organizers already have an event site, such as the Framer templates
[Confarex](https://confarex.framer.website/),
[Plenary](https://plenary-conference.framer.website/) and
[Conflux](https://conflux.framer.ai/), or
[Adobe 99U](https://event.adobe.com/adobe99u). Rather than asking them to
rebuild it, Presto would give them **one snippet**. Any button they mark with
`data-presto-book` then opens the **Booking Modal** over their page:

```html
<!-- 1. Anywhere on the page: the button (organizers can style it however they like) -->
<a href="https://book.presto.events/imex-america-2027" data-presto-book>Book your hotel</a>

<!-- 2. Once, before </body> -->
<script src="https://book.presto.events/embed.js" data-event="imex-america-2027" async></script>
```

- **No script, or the script is blocked?** The link still goes to the Presto
  booking page. It falls back cleanly.
- **With the script:** the click opens the modal, and the organizer's page stays put.
- **Optional:** `data-presto-stay="2027-09-27/2027-10-01"` fills in the stay
  dates, which is how "Book for this day" works on the agenda.

The **Widgets & Embeds** R&D area (Spartan × Eventpipe) already prototypes the
embed pattern in more depth. These concepts reuse the same idea for corporate events.
