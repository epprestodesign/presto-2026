## 1 · The booking flow

The same five steps as the main prototype, ending on the confirmation page:

| Step | What the guest does |
| --- | --- |
| **Landing** | Picks dates and "Book Reservations" (or "Hold Rooms" for a group) on the Baltimore event page. |
| **Browse Hotels** | Compares Baltimore-area hotels on a list and map. |
| **Hotel Details** | Picks a room and reserves it. |
| **Checkout** | Enters contact info and payment. |
| **Confirmation** | Sees the booking, **then the Experience Now offer**. |

All three flows get the offer: single reservation, multiple reservations and group block hold.

## 2 · Confirmation page

The offer sits **after the reservation summary and before the policies**.

- **Noticeable:** a tinted card with a colored edge, unlike the white summary cards around it.
- **Doesn't compete:** it comes after the booking details, uses smaller type than the summary, and has no big button.
- **Logo only:** a small XNow logo marks who the offer is from. The card keeps Presto colors and type, so it reads as a partner add-on, not an XNow ad.

See **Confirmation Page** (desktop + mobile).

## 3 · Confirmation email

The email order is: booking details → **View your reservation** button →
**Experience Now offer** → help and footer. The offer is a text link, not a
second button, so the main action stays clear. See **Confirmation Email**
(desktop + mobile).

## Notes from xnow.com

- The partner calls itself **XNow**; "ExperienceNow" is only a tagline.
- XNow discounts are for **verified groups** (students, teachers, healthcare workers, first responders, alumni). Not every guest will qualify, so "special offers" may overpromise.
- XNow also sells **hotels** ("Save up to 40%"), which could compete with the booking the guest just made.
